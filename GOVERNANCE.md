# Governance

## The Boundary

myEElab is divided into two distinct components:
- **`packages/core`**: The open-source client-side simulation engine, schematic editor, and netlist generator. This will remain free and open-source forever.
- **`packages/cloud`** & **`packages/web`**: The proprietary services providing cloud sync, auth, billing, AI assist, and the full application shell.

The boundary between what's free and what's paid is fixed. Changing this boundary after the community has built upon it destroys trust, which is far more expensive than any perceived lost revenue. Everything in `packages/core` stays free per the roadmap. 

## Contribution & Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

We welcome contributions to `packages/core`. All contributions are subject to a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO), which asserts that the contributor has the right to submit the code. 
All merges to the main branch require CI tests to pass.

## Decision Making

At this stage, governance follows a "Founder decides, but the roadmap and reasoning are public" model. Decisions are transparent so that no contributor invests time against a hidden plan.

## Support

The free tier is supported strictly via community/issue-tracker only. There is no SLA for free users. Paid tiers offer elevated support options.
